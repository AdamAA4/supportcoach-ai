Surface brief: practice call (src/app/call/page.tsx) — applies to the studio visual world across home, setup, call, and report.

## Scope and visitor mode

Operate mode. The trainee completes a voice practice call; the product lead reviews reports. All four screens share one visual world; the call screen is the reference surface.

## Audience, job, constraints

Support trainees rehearsing with a simulated customer grounded in a confirmed FAQ/policy snapshot; often on Android Chrome. Job: join, speak, get scored. Constraints: voice-only, preserved controls and test-pinned strings, mobile-first, AA contrast, no new dependencies, server-only secrets.
