| ⭐⭐⭐ | Open: importing a JavaScript-rendered FAQ page produces garbage facts | equityedge.io/faq renders its real Q&A content client-side; the server-side importer only sees the topic menu, contact form, and footer in the static HTML. The extractor pairs those labels together (e.g. "Q: How does the KYC verification work? / A: Slippage Restricted Countries..."), producing fake facts, wrong drills, wrong customer questions, and a meaningless accuracy report. The LLM-assisted path cannot help until the Vercel deployment has LLM_PROVIDER/LLM_API_KEY configured (they exist only in the local .env.local today), and even then the harvested corpus may not contain the answers. Product lead reproduced on the deployed build 2026-09-18. Candidate fixes: configure LLM env vars on Vercel and verify the grounding layer; discover and fetch the site's FAQ data API/embedded state server-side with the existing SSRF guardrails; or render with a headless browser (heaviest). Paste-the-text remains the safe workaround. |
