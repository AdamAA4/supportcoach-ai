ADAMTRADING Practice FAQ

Original company material for simulated support conversations.

Fictional demo only. ADAMTRADING is an invented training company. Every account, trade, balance and reward described here is simulated. This page does not offer brokerage services, investments, real-money trading or real payouts. These original rules are for SupportCoach AI practice, not another company's policies.
What is the profit target?

The demo evaluation requires a 10% profit on the starting account balance.

What is the daily loss limit?

The daily loss limit is 4% of the balance at the start of the UTC day. Both closed losses and open trading losses count toward this limit.

What is the total loss limit?

The total loss limit is 8% of the starting account balance. It is a fixed limit and includes both closed losses and open trading losses.

How many trading days are required?

The evaluation requires at least 5 separate trading days. A trading day counts when at least one trade is closed on that UTC day.

Is there a deadline to complete the evaluation?

There is no calendar deadline to complete the demo evaluation.

How much can I risk on one trade?

The maximum risk on a single trade is 1% of the starting account balance.

Which markets are available?

The simulator supports EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, XAUUSD and BTCUSD.

What leverage is available?

The demo leverage limits are 1:30 for forex, 1:10 for gold and 1:2 for cryptocurrency.

Can I trade around major news?

Opening or closing a trade is prohibited from 2 minutes before until 2 minutes after a high-impact news event affecting that trade's market.

Can I hold a trade overnight?

Overnight holding is allowed in the demo account.

Can I hold a trade over the weekend?

Weekend holding is not allowed. All open positions must be closed before Friday at 20:00 UTC.

Can I use an automated trading strategy?

Automated strategies are allowed when the strategy belongs to the trainee and does not exploit simulator errors.

Is copy trading allowed?

Copying trades between a trainee's own demo accounts is allowed. Copying another person's trades is prohibited.

Which trading practices are prohibited?

Price-feed manipulation, latency exploitation and coordinated opposite positions across accounts are prohibited.

What happens if a loss limit is breached?

The current demo evaluation ends when either loss limit is breached. The trainee must start a new demo evaluation.

When can I request my first simulated reward?

The first simulated reward request becomes available 7 calendar days after the demo evaluation is passed.

What is the simulated reward split?

The trainee receives 75% of the eligible simulated profit as demo reward credits.

What is the minimum simulated reward request?

The minimum simulated reward request is 50 demo credits. These credits have no cash value.

What must I check before requesting a simulated reward?

All positions must be closed and the demo account must have no active rule violations before a simulated reward request is submitted.

How long does a simulated reward request take?

A simulated reward request is reviewed within 2 business days.

How do I ask for a review of a flagged demo trade?

Use the simulator's support form and provide the demo account ID, the trade ID and the reason for the review.

Does ADAMTRADING require deposits or pay real money?

No deposits are required and no real-money rewards are paid. ADAMTRADING exists only as fictional training material.
